Ads.txt Manager

📄 What is app-ads.txt?

The app-ads.txt file tells ad networks which publishers are authorized to sell your ad inventory. It helps prevent ad fraud and is required by Google AdMob. The file must be hosted at the root of your developer website (e.g., hossamgamal.web.app/app-ads.txt).

Format: domain, publisher-id, relationship, certification-id

🌍 Connect a Custom Domain to Firebase Hosting

          

            Follow these steps to connect your own domain (e.g., yourdomain.com) to your site.
          


          

            1
            

              Open Firebase Console

              Go to Firebase Hosting Console → Click "Add custom domain"
            

          


          

            2
            

              Enter Your Domain

              Type your domain name (e.g., hossamgamal.com or www.hossamgamal.com)
            

          


          

            3
            

              Verify Ownership

              Firebase will give you a TXT record. Add it to your domain's DNS settings at your registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
            

          


          

            4
            

              Configure DNS Records

              Add the A records Firebase provides, pointing to their IP addresses. Remove any existing A records for that domain.
            

          


          

            5
            

              Wait for SSL

              Firebase automatically provisions a free SSL certificate. This can take up to 24 hours.
            

          

        


        

          
💻 Or Use Firebase CLI

          
Run this command in your project directory:

          
npx firebase-tools hosting:channel:deploy --project rooh-almuslim

          

            For custom domain setup via CLI, see the
            Firebase Hosting docs.
